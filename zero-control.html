About The Project

What happens when classic board games meet the digital world? A cyberpunk twist on Monopoly. In this fast-paced group project, we crafted the physical game universe together. My sole focus was designing the companion app — replacing messy paper money with a sleek, futuristic digital wallet that powers the game's economy.

What Is Cyberpunk?

Cyberpunk literature describes a relatively near dystopian future characterized by an alienated society and rapidly advancing technology. Bioengineered implants, large-scale genetic engineering, drug use, human-machine interfaces, as well as mega-corporations and mega-cities are among the narrative hallmarks of this literary genre. The classic cyberpunk narrative typically presents an anti-hero who is forced to fight against the system and usually manages to triumph in the end.

The Characters

The game focuses on five rebel characters and a gamemaster character — Zero, whose purpose is to hunt down the rebels and oversee the game. In the game, players complete missions and engage in battles among themselves and against Zero. We chose to give each character their own story to help players connect more deeply with the characters and add another layer of depth.

We chose to create the characters ourselves and enhance them using image generation tools like Firefly and Midjourney.

The Board

We built the board in hexagonal shapes, inspired by the Catan game and the idea of changing locations to create a game that can be revisited multiple times in different ways, ensuring that the game does not repeat itself. We chose a dark color palette characteristic of the cyberpunk world. We started with bright, neon color palettes, but after improvements, we decided to reduce the neon elements to avoid visual overload.

Each hexagon marks a target destination point on the board — shown here with both its front and back side.

The Cards

We chose to design the cards in a similar but simpler language to provide more space for card content. We created over 200 content cards with different categories. Additionally, we created resource cards that players will use in battles. For the gamemaster, we created a separate deck that will help him in battle — we created a team of villains, each with different abilities, whose goal is to defeat the rebels.

The Packaging

We wanted the packaging to capture attention and convey the game's atmosphere — creating a box that would make people want to pick it up off the shelf and take it home. We chose to feature Zero overlooking the city, effectively communicating both the game's name and its overall mood in a single, striking visual.

The App

With the physical game already rich in personality and physical touch, the app's job was to disappear into the background — a fast, glanceable digital wallet that tracks each player's balance and safe, without slowing the game down.
